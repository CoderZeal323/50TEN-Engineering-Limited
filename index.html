Who We Are

Engineering reliability into every installation.

50TEN Engineering Ltd is a technical services company built around electrical and instrumentation work: the wiring, panels, instruments, and control systems that decide whether a site actually runs or sits idle.

Most of our work sits at the point where design meets the field. That's installing and terminating instruments, testing and commissioning systems before handover, and getting called back in when a fault takes something down. On a site, an hour of downtime costs more than the fix usually does, so speed on that last part matters as much as accuracy on the first.

The 50TEN Engineering Academy started because we kept meeting graduates who understood the theory but had never touched a live panel. It trains people for the parts of the job that only show up once you're actually on site.

Vision

To be a leading name in electrical and instrumentation engineering across Africa's industrial and energy sectors.

Mission

Leadership

The Team

John Bright

Founder & Director, 50TEN Engineering Ltd

John Bright founded 50TEN Engineering to fill a gap he kept running into on site: too many plants lose time waiting on E&I work that should have been straightforward. The company was incorporated in Nigeria in June 2026 under the Companies and Allied Matters Act, with a scope covering electrical, instrumentation, automation, mechanical, and industrial engineering services. That range is deliberate, it means one call covers installation, testing, commissioning, and the maintenance work that follows, instead of juggling separate contractors for each stage.

The Academy grew out of the same instinct. John saw graduates leaving engineering school with theory but no field exposure, and built a training track to close that gap directly.
